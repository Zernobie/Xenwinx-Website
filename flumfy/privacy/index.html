Privacy Policy – Flumfy: Universe

Developer: Xenwinx Studio  |  Effective date: 12 October 2026  |  Contact: xenwinx7@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the Android game Flumfy: Universe (package name com.xenwinx.flumfy, the "Game") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The Game is published by Xenwinx Studio ("we", "us"), South Africa.

1. The short version

The Game does not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contact list, photos, location or any other personal details. Your game progress is stored only on your device. The Game shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob, and AdMob may collect certain device information to deliver and measure those ads, as described below.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress and settings (for example your high score, unlocked content and sound settings) in local storage on your device. We do not receive this data. It is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

3. Information collected through advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC, to show ads, including optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch in return for an in-game benefit. When an ad is requested or shown, AdMob may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (where available and permitted by your device settings);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and software information (such as device model, operating system version, language and app version);
  • information about ad interactions (such as whether an ad was shown, clicked or completed).

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. More information on how Google uses data from apps that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Children

The Game is designed to be suitable for a general audience, including children. We have therefore configured advertising in the Game to be treated as directed to children where required, which means personalised (interest-based) advertising is not used and only ad content and ad formats allowed for child audiences are requested. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at the email address above and we will delete it.

You can reset your device's advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation at any time in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use the Game to track you across other apps or websites ourselves.
  • We do not require or collect accounts, passwords or payment details. The Game does not contain in-app purchases.

6. Sharing of information

We do not receive personal information through the Game, so we have nothing to share. Advertising data described in section 3 is collected directly by Google AdMob and is shared with Google and its advertising partners as described in Google's policies.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the Game or clear its storage. Data held by Google AdMob is retained under Google's own policies. Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to delete, but you may contact us at any time with a request.
